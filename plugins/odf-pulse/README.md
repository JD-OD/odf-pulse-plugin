# ODF Pulse Plugin for Claude

Let Claude work with your Pulse dashboards, deals, properties, buyers, broadcasts, CRM and marketing data through the Open DealFlow AI Connector.

## Requirements

- An active Open DealFlow Pulse subscription
- Claude with paid plan (Claude API or Claude web with Pro/Team subscription)
- The AI Connector enabled on your Pulse account

## Installation

1. **Add the marketplace** (Customize → Plugins → Add → Add marketplace → Add from a repository)
   - Paste: `https://github.com/JD-OD/odf-pulse-plugin`
   - Click "Add" to sync the marketplace

2. **Install the plugin** (Discover → Odf Pulse → Add)
   - Select the ODF Pulse plugin and add it

3. **Connect your account** (On the plugin page → Connectors → Connect)
   - Sign in to Pulse and enter the 6-digit code emailed to you

Once connected, Claude can read your dashboards, properties, deals, and CRM data. If "Connect" says the connector isn't enabled, contact support@opendealflow.com.

## What Claude can access

- Pulse dashboards and metrics
- Deal pipeline and kanban boards
- Properties and property details
- Buyer information
- Email broadcasts and workflows
- Your CRM: contacts, conversations, opportunities and more, for every account you administer
- Marketing data, when it is on your plan: ad spend and results, lead forms, webinar registration and attendance, and ROI by lead source

Claude can also make changes in your CRM, send messages and run bulk jobs. It shows you what it will do and asks before sending, changing many records or deleting anything.
